Step 2 · Your Background

A few guided prompts — not a blank text box:

  • "What's your professional background in 2-3 sentences?"
  • "What kind of work energizes you most?"
  • "What do you want in your next role that you don't have now?"
  • "Any deal-breakers?" (optional)

This feeds directly into Claude's dimension generation prompt

Step 3 · Claude generates your dimensions

Loading screen while Claude reads their answers and produces:

  • 5–8 custom scoring dimensions tailored to them
  • A name + description for each
  • Suggested default weights
  • A short "profile summary" paragraph Claude will use when scoring

Same structure as your current profile.json, generated fresh per user

Step 4 · Review & tune

User sees their generated dimensions with:

  • Inline editing — rename or rewrite any dim
  • Add a dimension / remove one
  • Weight sliders (same as your current Settings tab)
  • "Regenerate" button if they want Claude to try again

Always editable later from their Settings tab

Step 5 · Name your first board

Simple prompt:

"What do you want to call your first tracker board?"
e.g. "Tech roles", "My job search", "Fall 2026"

They can create more boards any time. This just seeds the first one.

After onboarding completes

Background job kicks off: scores all current jobs in the shared pool against their new profile. First time they land on the Jobs tab there'll be a progress indicator — "Scoring X jobs for you…" — and results populate as they come in.
