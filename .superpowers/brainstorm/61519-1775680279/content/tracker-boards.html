Section 4: Multiple Tracker Boards

Two options for how board-switching lives in the UI

A

Board switcher inside the Tracker tab

The bottom nav stays the same (Jobs / Tracker / Settings). Inside the Tracker tab, a dropdown or pill tabs at the top let you switch boards.

Pros

  • Nav stays clean — no extra tab
  • Feels like a filter, not a mode switch

Cons

  • Slightly buried if you switch boards frequently
B

Boards as a sidebar / drawer ⭐ Recommended

A slide-in sidebar (swipe or hamburger) shows all your boards. Tapping one loads it. Active board name shows in the Tracker tab header.
